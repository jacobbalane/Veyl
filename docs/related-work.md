# Related work

Coming in v0.1. Veyl builds on research into acoustic sensing, channel estimation and gesture
tracking. Appendix E of `VEYL_PLAN.md` lists the work to credit, including FingerIO, LLAP, Strata,
SoundWave, GCC-PHAT, the One Euro filter and the Audio EQ Cookbook.

Source links and careful descriptions will be added with the relevant implementation. Veyl's
distinction is using the sound already playing, with visible capture, paired devices and local
processing. No research result establishes Veyl's accuracy without its own device tests.
