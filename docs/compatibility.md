# Compatibility

Target baseline: Android 8.0+ and Windows 10 version 1809+ or Windows 11 on x64 and ARM64.
Copying other apps' sound on the phone requires Android 10+.

No Veyl device checks have been completed. The maintainer's playback-capture test in another app
is input to the design, not proof that Veyl works on that device.

Phone reports will include model, Android and Veyl versions, microphone setup, rate, sound-check
score, app compatibility and poke results. PC reports will include Windows version and processor,
speaker and microphone devices, raw mode, sound-check score and poke results.
