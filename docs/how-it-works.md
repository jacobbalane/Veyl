# How it works

Coming in v0.1. Veyl will compare the exact sound being played with what a microphone hears.
Sound reaches the microphone directly and then again as echoes from the desk, room and your hand.
The comparison reveals how strongly it returns at each delay.

Veyl will learn the echoes that stay still. Changes reveal a moving hand. A quick move toward the
phone or microphone and back will become a poke. The meter will show whether the current sound
is clear and bright enough for sensing. This foundation does not yet detect gestures.
