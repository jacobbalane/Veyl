# Placement

Coming in v0.1. Place your phone flat, screen up, in front of your monitor. Keep its speaker and
microphones uncovered. Start with the media volume around half and your hand 10–30 cm above it.

For PC mode, use speakers and keep the microphone near where you poke. On a laptop, begin about
20 cm in front of the screen and keyboard. Headphones cannot provide the needed echoes;
Bluetooth speakers add delay. Device tests will confirm the final placement advice.
