# 0002 — Foundation preview wording

Date: 2026-10-22. Status: accepted for M0.

## Context

The 0.0.1 preview opens both apps before listening, pairing or gestures exist. Appendix C describes
the finished v0.1 flow. A clear preview notice must explain why its controls are unavailable.

## Decision

Add the foundation strings in Appendix C.4 and `docs/ui-copy.md`. Show the same notice on desktop
and Android. Keep the browser preview badge, actions off and unavailable controls disabled.
The site labels its example meter and illustration as samples of the planned release.

## Consequences

The preview can be used to check layout and installation without suggesting sensing is ready.
Remove the foundation notice when the corresponding real behaviors pass their milestone gates.
