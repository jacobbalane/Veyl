# Getting started

First release coming soon. Sensing, pairing and actions are coming in v0.1.

With your PC, you will choose speakers and a microphone and play music or a video. With your
phone, you will install both apps, pair them on the same home network, place the phone flat in
front of the monitor, and start listening while sound plays through its speaker.

You will practise a poke before switching actions on. Exact installation and first-run steps
will follow device verification. See [progress](progress.md) for the current status.
