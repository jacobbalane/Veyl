You can also enter the address shown on your phone on the Phones tab.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Fix: pairing needs the app open | Pairing works only while Veyl is open on your phone's screen.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Fix: too many tries             | After several pairing tries that didn't finish, Veyl pauses pairing for a few minutes to keep other people out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Fix: pairing didn't finish      | Keep Veyl open on your phone while you pair, and check that both screens show the same code.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Fix: phone busy                 | Disconnect it on the other PC or close Veyl there, then connect again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Fix: couldn't verify            | Forget the phone on the Phones tab, then pair it again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Fix: versions differ            | Download the latest Veyl for both from the Veyl website.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Fix: screen locked              | Keep Veyl open on your phone while you use it, or set a longer screen timeout.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Fix: blocked click              | Windows doesn't let apps click in apps that run as administrator. Use Veyl with other apps, or open that app without administrator rights.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Fix: audio devices              | Reconnect the device or choose another one in Settings, and close apps that use it on their own.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
