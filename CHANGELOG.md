### New

- **Your phone becomes a sonar.** The Android app listens with its microphone to the echo of
  what it plays — YouTube and YouTube Music on Android 10 or later, your own music in the Veyl
  player, or Veyl's test sound — and sends it, with voices filtered out, to your PC.
- **Or use only your PC.** PC mode uses the PC's own speakers and microphone with any app.
- **Pair with a code.** Phone and PC find each other on your home network, pair by comparing a
  six-digit code, and talk over an encrypted link. "Add by address" helps when discovery is blocked.
- **Poke to click.** A quick poke toward the phone or microphone clicks where your pointer is,
  plays or pauses, skips a track or presses Space. Actions start off; switch them with the header,
  the tray or Ctrl+Alt+V. Veyl tells you when Windows blocks input to an app running as
  administrator.
- **See what Veyl hears.** The meter scores the current sound and says how to improve it, on the PC
  and on the phone's listening screen, which also flashes for each poke.
- **Test recordings on your PC.** Record a test, optionally marking pokes with Space, to check how
  well Veyl works. Recordings stay on the PC and are deleted after 7 days by default.
- **Get going in a few steps.** A first-run guide walks through the phone or PC setup and a
  practice poke; the poke test tells you how many of 10 pokes Veyl saw and what to try.
- **Help in the app.** Placement, a fix for every tip and error, privacy, diagnostics and About.
- Sensitivity, distance range, room temperature and the poke action apply immediately.
